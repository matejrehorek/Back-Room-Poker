Calculating…

Every possible board is counted when all hole cards are set. With random cards in play, the lab samples 200,000 deals instead, which lands within about 0.2% of the true figure.

Privacy

This site uses no cookies, analytics, ads or tracking, and the page makes no requests to other servers. Your table options are saved only in your own browser's local storage, so they are still set on your next visit. Clear this site's data in your browser to remove them. Nothing you do in the game leaves your device. Like any website, the hosting provider may record technical information such as IP addresses in its server logs to run and secure the service.
